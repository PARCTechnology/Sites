PARC Labs

TXSE Protocol Modeling, Order-State Research & Stream Reconciliation

PARC Labs is an independently developed simulation environment for studying exchange protocol behavior, binary message encoding, order-state transitions, session framing, and market-data workflows using publicly available technical specifications.

Current Lab Status: This environment is not connected to, sponsored by, or certified by the Texas Stock Exchange. FIX and SEED application-message behavior is modeled from public TXSE specifications. RAKE is represented only at the session/framing role level until its complete framing specification is loaded into the simulator. The PARC symbol and simulator identifiers shown below are local laboratory fixtures and do not represent live TXSE instruments.

01. FIX 5.0 SP2

Order-entry and drop-copy application messages modeled using TXSE-defined FIX tags, message types, enumerations, and exchange-specific fields.

02. SEED Binary Order Entry

Little-endian binary payload construction using TXSE field offsets, presence bits, fixed-width integers, bitfields, and scaled prices.

03. RAKE Session & Framing

Transport/session research for the payload-agnostic TCP/UDP framing layer used with protocols such as SEED and TXSE market-data feeds.
